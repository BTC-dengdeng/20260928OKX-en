**To: Trading Account**

Choose USDT or another asset and complete the internal transfer.

After that, open the BTC/USDT trading page.

Two common order types for beginners are:

### Market Order

A Market Order buys or sells using the best available liquidity in the market.

The main advantage is speed and simplicity.

However, during volatile conditions, your actual execution price may differ from the last price shown before you submit the order.

### Limit Order

A Limit Order allows you to set the price at which you want to buy or sell.

For example, if BTC is trading at 80,000 USDT but you only want to buy at 78,000 USDT, you can place a Limit Order at 78,000.

The order will only execute if market conditions reach a price that satisfies your order.

Open orders can usually be monitored in:

**Open Orders**

Completed or cancelled transactions can be reviewed under:

**Order History**
